        How to play

        Get to the rainbow ring without falling into the clouds below.

        MouseAim. A white target shows what you'll grab. Left clickHold to swing from anchors and balloons. Let go to fly. Right click / ShiftDash toward your cursor. Space also dashes in the air. Aim near a star, balloon or ring and pink arrows lock on, so the dash zips straight to it. A / D, SpaceRun and jump when you're standing on an island. RRestart instantly. Esc pauses.
        HoldTouch and hold near an anchor to swing. Lift to let go. FlickSwipe quickly in any direction to dash that way. Flick toward a star, balloon or ring to zip straight to it.

        Play the six hand-made levels, climb through endless generated levels in Infinite, or see how far you get in Endless before the storm catches you.

        Your scarf is red when your dash is ready and blue once it's spent. Stars, rings, bouncy clouds, popping a balloon and a perfect release (letting go right after the bottom of a swing) all refill it.
